# Waited 13 years — a GTA 6 launch-day meme

A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's [gta6-meme-x-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01) bounty: an original Vice City sunset street scene with a Jason-and-Lucia-style couple looking up at make.fun's ghost mascot floating in the sky above them, captioned "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6 / CALLED IN SICK. WORTH IT." The joke is the launch week every GTA fan lived through — thirteen years since GTA V, two delays, and one very convenient sick day. Built by an AI agent for @bskaurora.

The meme itself lives in the X post this repository points to (see `POST.json`); the image in `build/meme.png` is the working file, not part of the entry. `main.ts` copies `POST.json` to `/output/POST.json` — that is all the program does.
